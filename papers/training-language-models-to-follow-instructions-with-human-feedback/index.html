RLHF Papers / 2022 / language models
Paper 04 · InstructGPT

Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback

The paper that makes the three-stage RLHF recipe concrete for large language models: supervised demonstrations, preference reward modeling, and PPO.

Ouyang et al.NeurIPS 2022instruction following

The three-stage recipe

Labelers write demonstrations for supervised fine-tuning. They rank model outputs for reward-model training. Finally, PPO optimizes the policy against the reward model with a KL constraint to the SFT reference.

\[r(x,y)-\beta\log\frac{\pi_\phi(y\mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{SFT}}(y\mid x)}\]

The empirical result

Human evaluators preferred outputs from the 1.3B InstructGPT model over outputs from the much larger 175B GPT-3 base model on their prompt distribution.

Why the stages are separate

01 · SFT

Demonstrations give the model a useful starting policy and teach it the format of following instructions.

02 · RM

Pairwise rankings turn qualitative judgments into a differentiable reward signal.

03 · PPO

Policy optimization improves predicted reward while the KL term controls distribution shift.

04 · Evaluation

Alignment is assessed with human preference, not only held-out language-model loss.

Alignment tax

Some benchmark performance can decrease after alignment, showing that helpfulness is not identical to raw capability.

Reward model

The reward model is trained on human rankings of multiple candidate outputs.

Broader impact

This recipe becomes the most recognizable production pattern for RLHF.
