Paper 05 · Beyond human harmlessness labels

Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback

Use a written set of principles to make the feedback process more scalable, inspectable, and less dependent on direct human judgments about harmful outputs.

Bai et al.AnthropicAI feedback

The constitutional loop

A model critiques its own response using a principle, revises it, and produces preference data. That data can then support preference optimization, including RL from AI feedback.

\[y' = \mathrm{Revise}(y\mid c),\qquad y^+\succ y\]

What changes?

The “human preference” source becomes a combination of human-written principles and model-generated critiques, revisions, and comparisons.

Method anatomy

01 · Constitution

Write principles that describe desired behavior, such as being helpful, honest, and harmless.

02 · Critique

Ask the model to identify where a response violates a principle.

03 · Revision

Ask it to produce a revised response that addresses the critique.

04 · Preference training

Use original/revised pairs to train a preference model or policy.

Scalability

AI-generated feedback can reduce the volume of direct human harmlessness comparisons.

Transparency

Principles make some of the intended behavior explicit instead of hiding it in labels.

Trade-off

The feedback model can inherit blind spots from the constitution and the model doing the critique.
